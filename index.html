How to play

Listen closely, type what you hear, and see how many you can get right.

  • Play the audio to hear the word and its related information.
  • The prompt loops automatically, so you can listen again.
  • Type and submit whenever you’re ready—even while audio is playing.
  • Answer the prompts in any order.
  • Each word allows one submitted attempt.
